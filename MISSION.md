# Mission

Starting 24 November 2026, a human owner is testing whether AI-assisted entrepreneurship can cover the AI subscription. December 2026 is the first monthly accounting period. Success means at least **US$20 net and withdrawable** from work attributable to the ChatGPT edition.

Prefer a useful product that reaches a paying customer quickly. Keep additional operating spend at US$0 and minimise demands on the owner's shared 30 minutes a day. Public evidence and the owner's verified aggregate records guide commercial decisions. The owner retains control of business decisions, accounts, spending and publication.
