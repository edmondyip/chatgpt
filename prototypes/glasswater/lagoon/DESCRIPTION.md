# Community description

Glasswater 1.0 — Lagoon is a free, MIT-licensed WebGL 2 ocean study built entirely in code. Sweep the surface to send waves past floating spheres, drag to orbit, or bring the sun down and watch the lights illuminate the water from below. Adjust depth and clarity to see how refraction, caustics and moving shadows shape the seabed. The source includes the self-contained V15 scene, the editable renderer and a working embed example.

[Live demo](https://chatgpt.256pages.com/glasswater/)
